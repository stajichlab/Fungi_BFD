# Fungal telomere features in BFD assemblies

## Observation
*Neurospora crassa* OR74A (ASMID `GCA_009805915.1_ASM980591v1`) carries canonical
fungal telomeres at the 5' ends of its two largest scaffolds (`NC_026507.1`,
`NW_011929460.1`). Both tracts match the reverse-complement monomer `CCCTAA`
(i.e., the canonical `TTAGGG` telomere repeat read on the minus strand at the
chromosome 5' end), with:
- 27–30 repeats per tract
- 162–180 bp per tract
- 342 bp total telomeric length and 57 repeats summed across both scaffolds

## Validation
Detected by the new `nextflow/bin/find_telomeres.py` pipeline step (regex mode,
default fungal monomer set, canonical 5'/3' orientation, terminal 5 kb search
window, ≥3 repeats, ≥36 bp). Manually inspected the reported `tract_seq` and
`flank_seq`; both tracts are terminal (`terminal=True`) and the flanking
sequence transitions cleanly from telomeric repeat into unique subtelomeric
sequence.

## Implications
- The canonical `TTAGGG`/`CCCTAA` repeat is present and detectable in at least
  this Sordariales reference genome, consistent with TeloBase's fungal
  monomer compilation.
- A merged `tables/telomeres.parquet` summarizing total telomeric length and
  repeat counts per genome is generated by `MERGE_TELOMERES`.

## Correction (2026-08-04)
The original validation above only checked 2 tracts, both at the 5' end, and
declared the tool "ready for kingdom-wide application" — but this masked a
severe bug: 3'-end detection was structurally broken (reversed the sequence
without complementing it, then searched with a reverse-complemented pattern),
and the `terminal` check required an exact 0-bp match with no tolerance,
silently dropping genuinely-terminal telomeres that had a few non-canonical
leading bases. Confirmed on 5 real genomes: 100% of all hits were 5'-end-only
before the fix. On `GCA_900073075.1_CML3066.v2` (*F. graminearum*, a real
4-chromosome telomere-to-telomere assembly), the buggy tool found 1 of 8
visibly-obvious real telomeric ends. Fixed in `find_telomeres.py` (see
`.living/decisions.md` "H" and `.living/learnings.md`, 2026-08-04) — re-run
now finds 7/8 real ends on that genome (the 8th genuinely lacks a repeat).
Any telomere-derived statistics generated by the pipeline before this fix
should be treated as unreliable and regenerated.
